## Photo and icon credit rules

- Photographs fetched from Wikimedia Commons are credited in the page's
  single footer credit line, with their licence, as the skill requires
  (`scripts/photos.mjs credits`). Never pass a stock photograph off as the
  client's product, people, or place.
- Brand icons are trademarks. Use only marks the client may use, and use the
  real full-colour mark as fetched — never a drawn look-alike, never redrawn
  from primitives.
- Library photographs used as stand-ins are credited in the footer and are
  slots the client's own photographs replace.
